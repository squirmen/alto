V5.1 · Restoring crown associations

8 October 2026. This release reconnects existing records to existing crowns. It does not redraw the canopy or establish new tree growth.

What changed

We restored 4,661 associations across 4,362 crowns: 3,518 Auckland Transport records, 470 Council Parks records and 673 earlier LiDAR records. Original IDs, source coordinates and survey histories remain intact. Former generated representatives are retained as linked records.

V5 required the source point to fall inside the filled polygon. Small positional offsets and holes could therefore leave a longstanding tree record unlinked while a new detection represented its crown. V5.1 also considers strong continuity between its earlier and current crown, a small edge distance, compatible height and the absence of competing crowns or conflicting recorded taxa.

What a link means

A restored association is spatial evidence, not proof that all records within a shared canopy describe one organism. Ambiguous cases remain for review: 28,735 audited candidates were not automatically attached. “Crown unlinked” does not mean a tree is missing or dead.

Consistent records and estimates

Map markers, profiles, property summaries, totals and KYTE nearby suggestions use the repaired snapshot. A current crown contributes one set of modelled service estimates. Those scenarios use the same V5 equations and retain their existing uncertainty. The two previously accepted KYTE visits remain available.

Polygon work comes next

The polygon geometry is unchanged, including known holes and clipped edges. The next geometry candidate will use native-resolution LINZ imagery, the LiDAR returns and researcher-drawn reference crowns. It must distinguish erroneous gaps from real gaps, and crown shape from source-record identity. Existing V6 growth-form research does not itself redraw crowns.
