V6 · Growth form and smoother crowns

This release adds a growth-form estimate for every V5 crown: whether a crown looks like an evergreen or deciduous broadleaf, a conifer, or a palm, cabbage tree or tree fern. It also redraws each crown with a smooth outline. Every growth form is a model estimate from the 2024 laser survey and aerial imagery, never an identified species: where the Council or another source recorded a species, that name stays the tree’s species and the model’s growth form is shown beside it.

A growth form is shown only where calls of its kind were right often enough when tested on crowns whose growth form is known from the Council register or from labelling by eye: at least 8 in 10 for a firm call, and at least 6 in 10 for a “possibly” call. Otherwise the crown reads “Growth form not yet determined”. On the laser surveys flown in April and October, when deciduous trees are in leaf, only evergreen broadleaf calls and palms seen by the palm detector are shown. “Likely deciduous (bare in late-autumn LiDAR)” is used outside the central isthmus only where the laser survey was flown in late June; elsewhere a deciduous call reads “Broadleaf (model)”.

How it was estimated

A gradient-boosted classifier (A2) reads the crown’s laser-return structure, its surroundings and image features from the 2024 aerial photograph at the crown top. It was trained on about 22,000 crowns labelled from the Council register and by hand, including 1,600 outside the isthmus, and checked on 600 fresh crowns it never saw. A separate palm detector confirms palms it can see from above. A variant trained to cope with leaves on the trees (A2_robust) scores crowns on April and October flights. A palm detection on a crown wider than 10 m is not used on its own, because such crowns are often several trees.

Smoother crowns

V5 traced crowns along the 0.5 m grid of the laser survey, so they looked square. V6 draws each crown with a smooth outline around the same cells: the crown’s area, height and every estimate are unchanged, and the outlines of neighbouring crowns do not overlap. Crowns drawn from a record in V5.2 are smoothed the same way.

Services

Service estimates use the growth form where a call is shown: leaf area and the trunk-size curve are weighted by the model’s probabilities, and the rest follows the call. Where the growth form is not yet determined, the estimate assumes an evergreen broadleaf tree, as before. The annual value estimate changes by +NZ$3,186,004, stored carbon by −114,533 t CO₂e and avoided runoff by −483,669 m³ a year; every change is attributed by cause. Most of it comes from crowns now called conifers that the previous release valued as evergreen broadleaf trees.
