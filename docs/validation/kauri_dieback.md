# Kauri dieback surveillance

Auckland Council has surveyed 52,047 kauri for dieback, recording a soil-sample result for the pathogen Phytophthora agathidicida and a field symptom assessment at each point. The overlay classifies each survey into a plain status tier.

The pathogen was confirmed present at 219 points (0.4%); a further 5,524 kauri (10.6%) showed possible or severe dieback symptoms in the field. The remainder were non-symptomatic or carried no status.

| Status | Points | Share |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Pathogen confirmed | 219 | 0.4% |
| Symptomatic | 5,524 | 10.6% |
| No symptoms recorded | 39,703 | 76.3% |
| Other or not assessed | 6,601 | 12.7% |

Surveillance is concentrated where kauri grow, so the points are not an even sample of the region. The overlay reports recorded status; it is not a prediction of spread. Source: Auckland Council kauri dieback public surveillance.
